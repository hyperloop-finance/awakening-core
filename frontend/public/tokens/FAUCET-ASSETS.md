# Faucet identifiers

BTC, USDC and USDT reuse the application's existing logo assets. Tesla and NVIDIA vector identifiers are sourced from https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/tesla.svg and https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/nvidia.svg (downloaded 2026-10-17). They identify the underlying companies only, not official bStocks or issuer-backed tokens.

All five faucet assets are simulated, valueless BNB Testnet tokens. Their verified contract addresses and claim receipts are recorded in `src/lib/contracts/faucet-deployment.json` and `contracts/deployments/faucet-bsc-testnet-97.json`.
